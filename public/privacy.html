隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-10-14

圖個方便 | PicShare（以下簡稱「本站」）是讓使用者分享與收藏 Unsplash 照片的社群網站。本政策說明我們收集哪些資料、如何使用、保存多久，以及你可以如何處理自己的資料。

1. 我們收集的資料

  • 帳號資料：註冊時提供的姓名（暱稱）與 Email（以小寫儲存）。密碼只以不可逆的雜湊值儲存，我們看不到你的原始密碼。
  • 你在本站產生的內容：你分享的照片（Unsplash 照片 ID、圖片網址、攝影師資訊與你輸入的分類名稱）、你建立的收藏庫與收藏紀錄。
  • 登入狀態與偏好：登入後，瀏覽器的 localStorage 會存放登入憑證（24 小時有效）與你選擇的語言。本站不使用 Cookie，也沒有廣告或第三方追蹤分析工具。
  • 技術資料：為防止濫用，伺服器會依來源 IP 位址限制請求頻率；主機與雲端服務平台也可能產生一般系統日誌（例如連線時間、IP 位址、錯誤訊息）。

2. 使用目的

提供註冊、登入、分享、分類、收藏等會員功能；寄送重設密碼信件；防止濫用並維護服務安全；以及遵守 Unsplash API 的相關規定。我們不會販售你的個人資料。

3. 第三方服務

  • Unsplash：本站的伺服器會向 Unsplash API 查詢照片。你在「找圖片」輸入的搜尋關鍵字、你分享的照片網址，會由本站伺服器傳送給 Unsplash。照片以 Unsplash 提供的網址直接顯示，你的瀏覽器會向 Unsplash 的伺服器載入圖片，Unsplash 因此可能取得你的 IP 位址等一般連線資訊；本站也可能依 Unsplash API 規定向其回報照片的使用事件。詳見 Unsplash 隱私權政策。
  • Brevo：寄送重設密碼信件時，你的 Email 會交給電子郵件服務供應商 Brevo 代為寄出。
  • 雲端主機服務：本站資料儲存在雲端服務供應商提供的主機與資料庫。

4. 資料保存與刪除

  • 你可以在「個人資料」頁的「危險區域」自行刪除帳號。刪除時會一併刪除你的帳號資料、你分享的照片、你的收藏庫與收藏紀錄，以及其他使用者對你所分享照片的收藏紀錄。
  • 例外：若管理者因違規或版權爭議移除你分享的照片，本站會留存一筆稽核紀錄（照片資訊、原分享者的 ID 與名稱、操作的管理者、原因與時間）。這筆紀錄不會因為你刪除帳號而移除，僅用於處理違規與版權爭議。
  • 你可以隨時在「個人資料」頁修改暱稱，或在「修改密碼」頁更新密碼。
  • 系統日誌依主機平台設定的期限保存，期限到後自動清除。

5. 你的權利

依你所在地的法律，你可能享有查詢、更正、刪除、限制處理你的個人資料等權利。除了上述自助功能，你也可以寄信到下方信箱提出請求，我們會盡快回覆。

6. 資料安全

我們採取合理的技術與管理措施保護資料，例如密碼只存雜湊值、限制登入與重設密碼的嘗試次數、管理功能僅限管理者使用。但網路傳輸與儲存無法保證絕對安全，請妥善保管你的密碼。

7. 政策變更

本政策若有變更，會更新本頁並修改「最後更新」日期。繼續使用本站，視為你接受更新後的政策。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

PicShare (“we”, “the site”) is a community site where people share and collect photos from Unsplash. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and what you can do with your data.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: the name (nickname) and email you give when signing up. The email is stored in lowercase. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes; we cannot see your original password.
  • Content you create: photos you share (Unsplash photo ID, image URL, photographer info and the categories you type), and the collections and saved items you create.
  • Login state and preferences: after you log in, your browser’s localStorage holds a login token (valid for 24 hours) and your language choice. We do not use cookies, advertising, or third-party tracking tools.
  • Technical data: to prevent abuse, the server rate-limits requests by source IP address. Our hosting and cloud platform may also keep ordinary system logs (such as connection time, IP address and error messages).

2. How we use it

To provide sign-up, login, sharing, categories and collections; to send password-reset emails; to prevent abuse and keep the service secure; and to follow the Unsplash API rules. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party services

  • Unsplash: our server queries the Unsplash API for photos. The search keywords you enter in “Find photos” and the photo URLs you share are sent by our server to Unsplash. Photos are displayed directly from URLs provided by Unsplash, so your browser loads images from Unsplash’s servers and Unsplash may receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address. We may also report photo usage events to Unsplash as its API rules require. See the Unsplash Privacy Policy.
  • Brevo: when we send a password-reset email, your email address is passed to our email provider Brevo to deliver it.
  • Cloud hosting: the site’s data is stored on servers and databases provided by a cloud hosting provider.

4. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account yourself in the “Danger zone” on the Profile page. This also deletes your account data, the photos you shared, your collections and saved items, and other users’ saved items that point to your shared photos.
  • Exception: if an administrator removes a photo you shared because of a violation or copyright dispute, we keep an audit record (photo info, the original sharer’s ID and name, the administrator, the reason and the time). This record is not removed when you delete your account and is used only to handle violations and copyright disputes.
  • You can change your nickname on the Profile page or your password on the Change Password page at any time.
  • System logs are kept for the period set by the hosting platform and then deleted automatically.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. Besides the self-service features above, you can email us at the address below and we will reply as soon as we can.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as storing only password hashes, limiting login and password-reset attempts, and restricting admin functions to administrators. No transmission or storage is completely secure, so please keep your password safe.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date. Continuing to use the site means you accept the updated policy.
