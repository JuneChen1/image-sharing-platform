隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-24

圖個方便 | PicShare（以下簡稱「本站」）是讓使用者上傳、分享與收藏照片的社群網站（也保留部分來自 Unsplash 的照片）。本政策說明我們收集哪些資料、如何使用、保存多久，以及你可以如何處理自己的資料。

1. 我們收集的資料

  • 帳號資料：註冊時提供的姓名（暱稱）與 Email。密碼只以不可逆的雜湊值儲存，我們看不到你的原始密碼。
  • 你在本站產生的內容：你上傳的圖片檔案，以及你填寫的分類、備註、「是否為原創作品」的標示與來源連結；你分享的 Unsplash 照片（照片 ID、圖片網址、攝影師資訊與你輸入的分類名稱）；你建立的收藏庫與收藏紀錄。你上傳的圖片、分類、備註與來源連結會公開顯示，任何人（包含未登入的訪客）都看得到。
  • 上傳圖片的處理：為控制檔案大小與保護隱私，本站會把你上傳的圖片轉成 WebP 格式、必要時縮小尺寸，並移除圖片中的拍攝資訊（EXIF，例如拍攝位置、裝置型號）。我們只保存處理後的檔案，不保存你的原始檔案。
  • 登入狀態與偏好：登入後，瀏覽器的 localStorage 會存放登入憑證（有使用期限，到期後需重新登入）、你的暱稱與你選擇的語言。本站不使用 Cookie，也沒有廣告或第三方追蹤分析工具。
  • 技術資料：為防止濫用，伺服器會依來源 IP 位址限制請求頻率；主機與雲端服務平台也可能產生一般系統日誌（例如連線時間、IP 位址、錯誤訊息）。

2. 使用目的

提供註冊、登入、上傳與分享圖片、分類、收藏等會員功能，儲存並顯示你上傳的內容，寄送重設密碼信件，防止濫用並維護服務安全，以及遵守 Unsplash API 的相關規定。我們不會販售你的個人資料。

3. 第三方服務

  • Unsplash：本站的伺服器會向 Unsplash API 查詢照片。你在「找圖片」輸入的搜尋關鍵字、你分享的照片網址，會由本站伺服器傳送給 Unsplash。照片以 Unsplash 提供的網址直接顯示，你的瀏覽器會向 Unsplash 的伺服器載入圖片，Unsplash 因此可能取得你的 IP 位址等一般連線資訊；本站也可能依 Unsplash API 規定向其回報照片的使用事件。詳見 Unsplash 隱私權政策。
  • 內容傳遞網路（CDN）：本站頁面的樣式與互動元件（Bootstrap）由 jsDelivr 提供，你的瀏覽器會向 jsDelivr 取得這些檔案，對方因此可能取得你的 IP 位址等一般連線資訊。本站只用它傳送程式庫檔案，不用於追蹤分析。
  • Cloudflare：本站使用 Cloudflare 提供網域解析（DNS）、內容傳遞與安全防護。連到本站的流量（包含你送出的內容）會經過 Cloudflare 的伺服器處理，Cloudflare 因此可能取得你的 IP 位址等一般連線資訊。詳見 Cloudflare 隱私權政策。
  • Brevo：寄送重設密碼信件時，你的 Email 會交給電子郵件服務供應商 Brevo 代為寄出。
  • 你填寫的來源連結：上傳非原創作品時，你填寫的來源連結會顯示給其他使用者；點擊後會前往第三方網站，該網站有自己的隱私權政策，本站無法控制。
  • 雲端主機服務：本站的資料、你上傳的圖片檔案，都儲存在雲端服務供應商提供的主機、資料庫與檔案儲存空間。

4. 資料保存與刪除

  • 你可以在「個人資料」頁的「危險區域」自行刪除帳號。刪除時會一併刪除你的帳號資料、你分享的照片（包含你上傳的圖片檔案）、你的收藏庫與收藏紀錄，以及其他使用者對你所分享照片的收藏紀錄。你也可以在「我的分享紀錄」取消分享單張照片，該照片與你上傳的檔案會一併刪除。
  • 圖片的暫存：為了加快載入速度，你上傳的圖片可能暫存在內容傳遞網路與瀏覽器的快取中，刪除之後最多約一天內才會完全消失。
  • 例外：若管理者因違規或版權爭議移除你分享的照片，本站會留存一筆稽核紀錄（照片資訊、原分享者的 ID 與名稱、操作的管理者、原因與時間）。這筆紀錄不會因為你刪除帳號而移除，僅用於處理違規與版權爭議。
  • 你可以隨時在「個人資料」頁修改暱稱，或在「修改密碼」頁更新密碼。
  • 系統日誌依主機平台設定的期限保存，期限到後自動清除。

5. 你的權利

依你所在地的法律，你可能享有查詢、更正、刪除、限制處理你的個人資料等權利。除了上述自助功能，你也可以寄信到下方信箱提出請求，我們會盡快回覆。

6. 資料安全

我們採取合理的技術與管理措施保護資料，例如密碼只存雜湊值、限制登入與重設密碼的嘗試次數。但網路傳輸與儲存無法保證絕對安全，請妥善保管你的密碼。

7. 政策變更

本政策若有變更，會更新本頁並修改「最後更新」日期。繼續使用本站，視為你接受更新後的政策。

8. 聯絡我們

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

PicShare (“we”, “the site”) is a community site where people upload, share and collect photos (it also keeps some photos from Unsplash). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and what you can do with your data.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: the name (nickname) and email you give when signing up. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes; we cannot see your original password.
  • Content you create: the image files you upload and the categories, note, “original work” marking and source link you provide; Unsplash photos you share (photo ID, image URL, photographer info and the categories you type); and the collections and saved items you create. The images, categories, notes and source links you upload are displayed publicly and can be seen by anyone, including visitors who are not logged in.
  • How uploaded images are processed: to keep file sizes small and protect privacy, the site converts the images you upload to WebP, shrinks them if necessary, and removes shooting information (EXIF, such as location and device model). We keep only the processed file, not your original.
  • Login state and preferences: after you log in, your browser’s localStorage holds a login token (it expires after a set period, after which you need to log in again), your nickname and your language choice. We do not use cookies, advertising, or third-party tracking tools.
  • Technical data: to prevent abuse, the server rate-limits requests by source IP address. Our hosting and cloud platform may also keep ordinary system logs (such as connection time, IP address and error messages).

2. How we use it

To provide sign-up, login, uploading and sharing images, categories and collections; to store and display what you upload; to send password-reset emails; to prevent abuse and keep the service secure; and to follow the Unsplash API rules. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party services

  • Unsplash: our server queries the Unsplash API for photos. The search keywords you enter in “Find photos” and the photo URLs you share are sent by our server to Unsplash. Photos are displayed directly from URLs provided by Unsplash, so your browser loads images from Unsplash’s servers and Unsplash may receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address. We may also report photo usage events to Unsplash as its API rules require. See the Unsplash Privacy Policy.
  • Content delivery network (CDN): the site’s styling and interface components (Bootstrap) are served by jsDelivr, so your browser fetches these files from jsDelivr, which may receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address. We use it only to deliver library files, not for tracking.
  • Cloudflare: the site uses Cloudflare for domain name resolution (DNS), content delivery and security protection. Traffic to the site (including what you submit) passes through Cloudflare’s servers, so Cloudflare may receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Brevo: when we send a password-reset email, your email address is passed to our email provider Brevo to deliver it.
  • Source links you provide: when you upload work that is not your own, the source link you enter is shown to other users; clicking it takes them to a third-party site, which has its own privacy policy that we do not control.
  • Cloud hosting: the site’s data and the image files you upload are stored on servers, databases and file storage provided by a cloud hosting provider.

4. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account yourself in the “Danger zone” on the Profile page. This also deletes your account data, the photos you shared (including the image files you uploaded), your collections and saved items, and other users’ saved items that point to your shared photos. You can also unshare a single photo on “My shared photos”; that photo and the file you uploaded are deleted together.
  • Image caching: to load faster, the images you upload may be cached by the content delivery network and by browsers, so after deletion it can take up to about a day for them to disappear completely.
  • Exception: if an administrator removes a photo you shared because of a violation or copyright dispute, we keep an audit record (photo info, the original sharer’s ID and name, the administrator, the reason and the time). This record is not removed when you delete your account and is used only to handle violations and copyright disputes.
  • You can change your nickname on the Profile page or your password on the Change Password page at any time.
  • System logs are kept for the period set by the hosting platform and then deleted automatically.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. Besides the self-service features above, you can email us at the address below and we will reply as soon as we can.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as storing only password hashes and limiting login and password-reset attempts. No transmission or storage is completely secure, so please keep your password safe.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date. Continuing to use the site means you accept the updated policy.
